The solution

Every question gets the shape of agent it needs.

Not a wall of chatbots. Two shapes of agent, applied deliberately: a team where a question needs several angles argued and then picked apart, and a single specialist where it does not. Every agent is bound, when it is built, to the records it may read and the things it may do.
