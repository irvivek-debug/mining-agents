# ⛏️ Mining & Petroleum Agentic Suite Catalog

Autonomous agents and process classification models for Mining, Exploration, Upstream Petroleum, and Downstream Refining operations.

## 🏗️ Agent Capability Breakdown
- **Geological Exploration & Well Optimization Agent**: Analyzes seismic logs, core sample assays, and wellbore telemetry.
- **Autonomous Haul Fleet & Equipment Dispatch Agent**: Optimizes haul truck routes, tire wear, and fuel consumption in open-pit mines.
- **Upstream Asset Integrity Agent**: Monitors pipeline pressure drops, pump vibration, and cathodic protection.
- **Refinery Yield & Downstream Supply Chain Agent**: Balances crude slate feedstocks against market demand and cracker throughput.
